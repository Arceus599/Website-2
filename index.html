Chinese Cuisine

Dim Sum: A Cantonese tradition of small, bite-sized portions of steamed, baked, or fried dishes (such as har gow shrimp dumplings or siu mai) served with tea. Other Chinese foods are Peking Duck, Kung Pao Chicken (Gong Bao Ji Ding), Ma Po Tofu, Xiaolongbao, Zhajiangmian, Buddha Jumps Over the Wall, Hakka Noodles and Chilli Chicken.

Dumplings

Dumplings are a broad class of dishes made from pieces of cooked dough, often wrapped around a sweet or savory filling, or cooked as small dough balls on their own.

Food

Food is any substance consumed by a living organism to provide energy, growth, repair, and nutritional support. It can be raw, cooked, or processed, and usually comes from plants, animals, or fungi.

Pizza

Pizza is an Italian dish made of a flat base of leavened wheat dough topped with tomato, cheese, and various other ingredients, then baked at high temperatures. It originated in Naples, Italy, and is eaten worldwide as a restaurant meal, street food, delivery staple, or frozen food.

Garlic Bread

Garlic bread is a toasted or baked bread dish topped with garlic, butter or olive oil, and herbs such as parsley or oregano. It draws on older traditions like Italian bruschetta (toasted bread rubbed with garlic and olive oil) and developed into its modern buttery form in Italian-American cooking.
